Text layers and clip speed come to Edit Video: words in a font of your choice over a dark wash, fading in and out, and clips that play faster, slower, along a ramp or in reverse. The toolbar of a noodle jumps to either end of it.

**Download:** [Mac · Apple Silicon](https://nodebanana.app/download/mac) (macOS 13 or later) or [Windows · x64](https://nodebanana.app/download/windows) (Windows 10 or later). Installed Mac apps update themselves from this release. The Windows installer is unsigned, so SmartScreen warns on the first run; say **More info → Run anyway**. Bring your own provider keys: Node Banana calls the providers directly and their usage is billed to you by them.

## Text layers

+ Text, under the audio rows, or Add text in the Edit video view's media pane, puts words over the video. The text sits on a dark wash across the picture and fades in and out at its start and end. Text layers live on rows of their own above the video, where you move, trim, split, copy and delete them as you do audio clips. The rail sets the text, the font, the size, the weight, the colour, the position, the wash and the fades.

The font can be one of the app's own, a font file you add to the Fonts folder in your Node Banana folder, or a font installed on your computer once you allow it. The desktop app allows it.

Render and Frame to canvas burn the text layers in as the preview shows them.

## Clip speed

In the Edit video view and the node's editor, a video clip can play from 0.1× to 10×. Speed, in the rail under Volume, has a slider and presets at 0.25×, 0.5×, 1×, 2× and 4×. Ramp changes the speed along an ease curve, a bezier or points you draw on the clip's open row on the timeline. Reverse plays the clip backwards, and Blend frames blends neighbouring frames in slow motion. The clip's box, its filmstrip, the ruler and the clips after it follow its new length.

Render burns in each clip's speed, ramp and reverse, frame by frame, and Frame to canvas grabs the frame they show. A clip's sound rises and falls in pitch with its speed. At a constant speed above 4× or below 0.25× it has none.

## Noodles

The toolbar of any noodle you click has Jump to start and Jump to end, which move the view to that end of the noodle. The crosshair on a hidden noodle's toolbar is gone, because Jump to start does the same job. The toolbar has no label field now. You rename a noodle by double-clicking its label while it is hidden.

## Ease Curve node

- A click on a curve handle no longer clears the chosen preset.
- A steep custom curve follows its handles. Before, it could be up to 0.6 % off.
- The arrow keys move a focused handle, and the plot sits on neutral black instead of navy.

## Edits opened in 2.2.0

Edit files keep version 1, so Node Banana 2.2.0 still opens an edit with text layers or clip speeds. It shows no text layers and plays every clip at its own speed, and it drops both whenever it saves, renames, renders or duplicates that edit. On an Edit Video node, 2.2.0 also drops clip speeds whenever it writes the node's timeline.

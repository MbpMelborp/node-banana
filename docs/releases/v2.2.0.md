The Video Stitch node becomes Edit Video, with a full-screen editor on a timeline and a video editing view of its own; a long generation is never abandoned, and a provider's refusal says why.

**Download:** [Mac · Apple Silicon](https://nodebanana.app/download/mac) (macOS 13 or later) or [Windows · x64](https://nodebanana.app/download/windows) (Windows 10 or later). Installed Mac apps update themselves from this release. The Windows installer is unsigned, so SmartScreen warns on the first run; say **More info → Run anyway**. Bring your own provider keys: Node Banana calls the providers directly and their usage is billed to you by them.

## Edit Video

**An editor on the node.** The Video Stitch node is now **Edit Video**, and its card's button reads **Render**. Expand on the node, or **Open editor** on its card, opens a near-full-screen editor: the sequence previewed under the playhead with its controls in one row beneath it, a rail for the selected clip, and a timeline. Reorder clips on the timeline; trim each by dragging its edges or typing In and Out, snapping to the playhead and the clip beside it; split at the playhead; mute or lower a clip's own sound; and add videos from the asset library, grouped by project, with one search field and a filter menu. Selected clips are ringed in white, audio is drawn in grey, Loop sits in the header's … menu, and the timeline's top edge drags it taller or shorter. Done writes the timeline back to the node, Save (⌘S) does the same and saves the workflow while the editor stays open, and Render encodes exactly what it shows. A workflow that never opened the editor renders the same clips with the same sound rule.

**Stacked.** The timeline can be laid out Stacked, like After Effects' sequence layers: one row per clip, each starting a set time before the previous one ends (Overlap, 1.0 s by default) until a clip is placed by hand. Drag a clip's box anywhere: sideways to start it at any time, up or down to another row, or both at once; nudge it with ⌥← and ⌥→, or type its Start. The higher row is in front: with Crossfade on the picture dissolves and the sound crossfades where clips overlap; off, the clip above covers the ones below while every clip is heard. Clips can overlap or leave gaps, which play black while the music plays on. Line up puts every clip back a set time before the one above ends. Track stays the default and encodes as before.

**Audio clips.** The editor's audio is clips on rows under the video. The connected music is the first clip; **+ Audio**, under the last audio row, brings in audio files from the library, each on a new row at the playhead. Drag a clip in time or to another row, trim it, split it with S, mute it, copy and paste it, and give it its own volume curve with Fade in, Fade out and Flat. Audio after the last video frame is cut. A node whose audio is still just its music renders exactly as before.

**Select, copy and paste.** ⌘-click, ⇧-click or drag a box to select several clips, or ⌘A for all; drag, remove, mute, nudge or split them together. ⌘C, ⌘X, ⌘V and ⌘D copy, cut, paste and duplicate clips with their trims and sound, into the same node or any other Edit Video node.

**Frame to canvas.** An icon in the control row under the video puts the frame under the playhead on the canvas as an image node beside the Edit Video node, at the sequence's full size, including a crossfade between two clips in Stacked. The frame is kept in the asset library as an edited image, and one undo on the canvas takes the node away.

## The Edit video view

Bare **E**, or **Edit video** in the tab strip, opens a full-screen video editor for **edit projects**: timelines saved on their own in your Node Banana folder (under Edits), not tied to any node or workflow, that use videos and audio from every project. It opens on a list of your edits, newest first, beside the cover of the edit under the pointer; Enter opens one and New edit starts one. The library pane beside the timeline lists every project's videos and sounds, grouped by project, with one search field and a filter menu (kind, project, favourites, sort); click a clip to add it at the playhead or drag it to where it should go. Edits save automatically when "Save automatically" is on, or with ⌘S, and leaving the view keeps your undo history and playhead. **Render** encodes the edit with the same engine as the node and keeps the video in the asset library under the edit's name, with Show in Assets and Add to canvas.

## Generations

- **Long runs no longer time out at ten minutes.** A Kie or Comfy Router generation is polled for as long as the provider says it is running. A 90-minute safety net remains, takes one last look before it gives up, and then says the run may still be going rather than that it failed.
- **A provider's refusal says why.** When Comfy Router refuses a run (a content-policy refusal, say), the node shows the provider's own explanation instead of a bare code, and at once.
- **Pick a run up again.** A generate node keeps the provider's request while it waits, and keeps it when the app stops waiting or is cut off; it is saved with the workflow and in the desktop app's crash checkpoint. The node then says the run may still be going, shows the request id to copy, and offers **Pick up**, which collects the result into the carousel and the asset library like a normal run. Comfy Router keeps a result for 24 hours.

## Fixed

- **Annotate's Cancel and Done took no clicks on the desktop.** The annotation editor laid its header across the top of the window, in the desktop's drag region, so clicking Cancel or Done dragged the window instead. The editor is now a near-full-screen dialog below that band.
- **Edit Video follows the card's order.** Dragging clips into a new order on the node's card changed the filmstrip but not the video it made. The video now follows the card.
- **Rendered clips keep their timing and sound.** Each join lost a fraction of a frame, so the sound drifted later through a long video, and a clip whose sound had a different sample rate from the first came out silent. Clips now meet at their exact lengths and every clip's sound is converted into the mix.
- **Edit Video keeps every connection after clips are removed.** A later clip's connection could be hidden on the canvas while it still fed the video. The node now keeps a socket for every connected clip.
